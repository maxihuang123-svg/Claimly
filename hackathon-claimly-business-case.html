The bigger problem: insurers pay out more than they owe.

A missed exclusion, a misread limit, a repair bill nobody questioned, a fraud nobody spotted. Each slip is small. Across thousands of claims, it adds up.

What should have been paidPaid on top
8–10% more is paid out on motor and casualty claims than should have been, on average.

Even the best claims teams still lose 4–7%.

Part of this comes from claims decided against the policy by mistake: the wrong coverage, limit or deductible. That is the part Claimly checks.

Source: Arthur D. Little, “7 levers to excel in claims management” (2024). Figures are average value leakage: the gap between what should have been paid on a claim and what was actually paid.
